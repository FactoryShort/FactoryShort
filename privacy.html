1. Who we are

FactoryShorts is a personal tool operated by an individual to publish original educational short videos to TikTok accounts owned by that same individual. This policy explains what information the app handles when it connects to TikTok.

2. Information the app accesses

  • Authorization data from TikTok: when the account owner connects an account through TikTok Login, TikTok provides an access token and a refresh token, together with the open ID of that account. These are used only to upload and publish videos that the owner has created.
  • Basic profile information of the connected account (display name and avatar), only to show which account is connected.
  • Video content and metadata (title, description, hashtags) that the owner provides for publishing.

3. Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store or process personal data about other TikTok users, viewers, commenters or followers. It does not track users or use cookies or advertising identifiers.

4. How the information is used

Tokens and video content are used solely to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with third parties, and nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer, are never published, and are not shared. Videos are produced locally and uploaded directly to TikTok through its official API.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the account remains connected. The owner can disconnect the app at any time from TikTok (Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions), which revokes access immediately, and can delete the locally stored tokens at any time.

7. AI-generated content

Videos published with this tool that contain AI-generated images or voice are labeled as AI-generated content in accordance with TikTok's policies.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision.
