What it does

FactoryShorts is a small tool I use to publish my own original, educational short videos (history, geography, curiosities and personal-finance education) to TikTok accounts that I own and operate.

How it works
  • I connect my own TikTok account to the app using TikTok's official login (OAuth).
  • The app uploads videos that I produced, together with their title and description.
  • Every video that uses AI-generated images or voice is labeled as AI-generated content.
  • Scripts are fact-checked against reliable references before publishing.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or analyze data about other TikTok users.
  • It does not post on behalf of anyone other than the account owner.
  • It does not send automated comments, likes, follows or messages.
